# Traffic plan: Instagram → website → Kindle pre-order

**Starting point (28 Nov 2026, Windsor.ai):** @thel.ifeyoukeepplanning · 0 followers · follows 0 · 1 post (reach 0, 1 like) · bio link = website ✅.
**Rule:** existing content only (approved videos, foundation carousels, Highlight frames). No new concepts.

## Why most posts will reach almost no one at first
Instagram shows feed posts (photos, carousels) mainly to **followers**, and an account with 0 followers has none. **Reels** are recommended to **non-followers**, and **Trial Reels** go to non-followers only. So the first two weeks focus on (1) Reels, (2) the first 100 followers and (3) sending every visitor to one place: the website.

## The funnel
**Discover** (Reels, hashtags/keywords, comments on other accounts, collaborations) → **Profile** (bio + pinned posts + Highlights) → **Website** (quiz, One-Project Card, Kindle button) → **Amazon UK pre-order**.
