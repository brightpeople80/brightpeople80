# Gold standard for Instagram videos

Approved by the author on 28 November 2026, based on **01 · Museum of Unfinished Dreams**. Every new video copies this *recipe*, but never its *content* (see `ALREADY-USED.md`).

## 1. Test first
Before anything is generated, write a `BRIEF.md` and pass all eight points:
1. Professional role
2. Target audience
3. AIDA (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action), with timings
4. Purchase motivation
5. Competitive differentiation
6. One clear CTA
7. Brand constraints
8. Measurable commercial objective (numbers, a time window and where it is measured)

## 2. The structure that works (about 60 s)
| Part | Length | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | 0–6 s | A single cinematic image plus one line that names a feeling the viewer has. |
| **Recognition** | 6–21 s | 3–5 specific, everyday examples, one scene each, 2–4 s per scene. |
| **Relief** | ~3 s | Remove the shame before asking for anything. |
| **The turn** | ~10 s | The book's core idea, then one small action the viewer can take today. |
| **Book entrance** | at the line naming the book | A special-effect reveal of the real cover (never an AI-drawn cover). |
| **Offer** | ~13 s | Kindle £3.99 pre-order, paperback £9.99 coming soon, 23 January 2027, spoken **and** on screen. |
| **Website** | ~8 s | A specific, free reason to visit the website, shown on a phone with the address on screen. |

## 3. Production rules
- **Voice:** the author's HeyGen voice clone "Belinda" only. No background music. Avoid `[pause]` tags (they add noises); use full stops instead. Loudness −16 LUFS.
- **Look:** warm, cinematic, stylised 3D animation; one consistent character per video; navy, gold and cream palette. The character is a reader, never presented as the author.
- **Book on screen throughout:** the corner badge (cover + "Out 23 Jan 2027") from the first example scene to the reveal.
- **Captions:** every spoken line, white with gold keywords, inside the safe zone (y 230–1440).
- **Real assets for the product:** real front cover, the 3D paperback render and a real screenshot of the website.
- **Format:** 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC.

## 4. Fixed facts (never change)
*The Life You Keep Planning* · Belinda Bright N. · Kindle £3.99 (pre-order now, Amazon UK) · Paperback £9.99 (coming soon) · Out 23 January 2027 · thelifeyoukeepplanningbook.netlify.app

## 5. Never
Invent reviews, results, sales, awards or quotes; describe the quiz as a psychological test; reuse a scene, metaphor, hook or example already listed in `ALREADY-USED.md`.
