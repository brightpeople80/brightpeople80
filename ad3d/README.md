# "Stop planning. Start.": surreal 3D book ad

The cover's scribble bursts out of the book as a giant gold tangle, which straightens on the floor into the cover's arrow. A tiny figure walks out along it.

The brand at the top is the wordmark: BELINDA BRIGHT N. / THE LIFE YOU KEEP PLANNING (there is no logo file). At the bottom are the slogan and one line: Kindle pre-order on Amazon UK · Out 23 January 2027.
