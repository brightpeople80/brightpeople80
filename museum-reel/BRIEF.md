# Creative brief: Museum of Unfinished Dreams Reel (v3)

Every video for *The Life You Keep Planning* is tested against these eight points before it is made.

## 1. Professional role
Senior social-media strategist and content director for Belinda Bright N.'s debut book, producing faceless, cinematic short-form video for Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

## 2. Target audience
UK adults, mainly 25–45, who overthink, collect ideas (screenshots, notebooks, courses), restart often, fear choosing the wrong path or struggle to follow through. They are not lazy; they are stuck between preparing and doing.

## 3. AIDA
| Stage | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Attention** | 0–6 s | A reader stands among glowing screens of her unfinished dreams. "Welcome to the Museum of Unfinished Dreams." |
| **Interest** | 6–21 s | Four exhibits the viewer recognises: the January notebook, the business name, the paused course, the saved folder. "None of them failed." |
| **Desire** | 21–43 s | The turn: "You don't need another plan. You need a way to begin." She chooses one dream and starts. **The book makes a special-effect entrance** on "That's what my book is all about." |
| **Action** | 43–64 s | Kindle £3.99 (pre-order now), paperback £9.99 (coming soon), launch day 23 January 2027, then **the website**: the free quiz "Which pattern is keeping you stuck?" Link in bio. |

## 4. Purchase motivation
- **Self-recognition:** each exhibit mirrors something the viewer owns.
- **Relief:** "None of them failed" removes shame before asking for anything.
- **Low price, low risk:** £3.99 Kindle pre-order, delivered on launch day.
- **A first step for free:** the website quiz gives value before any purchase.
- **Timeliness:** a fixed launch date (23 January 2027) gives a reason to act now.

## 5. Competitive differentiation
Most faceless motivation pages sell other people's courses with generic quotes. This book is:
- a practical tool, not a pep talk: the SELECT method, a 30-day follow-through programme that allows for missed days, the One-Project Card and the Action and Evidence Log;
- about choosing *one* idea, not doing more;
- written by the author, with a free self-reflection quiz on her own website.

## 6. Clear CTA
**One action: "Tap the link in my bio."** The bio link should open the website, which has both the Kindle pre-order button and the free quiz, so every viewer lands in one place. (Today the video names both; the bio link resolves them to one tap.)

## 7. Brand constraints
- Colours: dark navy, warm gold, white, soft cream. Fonts: Montserrat, Source Serif 4 italic.
- Faceless: the animated reader is a character, never presented as the author.
- Voice: the author's own (HeyGen clone "Belinda"); British English; warm, honest, practical; no hype.
- Never alter title, author, prices or date. Kindle £3.99 pre-order; paperback £9.99 **coming soon**; out 23 January 2027.
- No invented reviews, results, sales or awards. The quiz is described as self-reflection, not a psychological test.
- Captions inside Instagram's safe zone (nothing important below y≈1440 or above y≈230).

## 8. Measurable commercial objective
Within 14 days of posting:
- **150 link-in-bio taps** (Instagram profile link clicks, read through Metricool or Windsor.ai);
- **30 Kindle pre-orders** attributable to the campaign window (KDP pre-order report, compared with the previous 14 days);
- **35% average watch-through** on the Reel (Instagram insights).
These are starting targets for a new account and will be revised after the first review.
