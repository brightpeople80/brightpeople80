# Phase 3: Foundation posts and Highlights

Everything in `out/` is ready to upload. Feed posts are 1080×1350 (4:5), and Highlight frames are 1080×1920 (9:16).
To re-render after editing `src/frames.html`, run `node render.js`.

## Order of work

1. Publish the three posts in this order: Museum, then Planning Trap, then Discover the Book. On the grid, the last one posted shows first.
2. Pin all three. Tap the post, then **…**, then **Pin to your profile**. Pin Discover the Book last so it sits first.
3. Post each Highlight's story frames, add them to a new Highlight, and set the matching cover (`hl-cover-*.jpg`).

In Metricool: go to **Planning**, then **Create post**, click the **multimedia icon**, then **Drive**, and select the slides in numbered order.
